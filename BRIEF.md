# Procis.ai showcase microsite: build brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The owner's words: "make it fucking gorgeous", "it needs to sell itself", "only best in class". Copy is the owner's final text in `../drafts/site-copy.md`, used verbatim (lines split for layout only).

## The eight topics

1. **Who it is for.** The head of procurement at a global consumer-goods company (reference: Unilever). Sceptical, numerate, short of time, and buried in supplier increase letters. Their CFO will read whatever they forward.
2. **What they should do.** Book a teardown (mailto:hello@procis.ai). The secondary action is "See a claim checked", which jumps to the peak. There is one call to action on the page, repeated in the nav and at the end.
3. **What they should believe.** A price increase is a claim that can be tested line by line, and Procis does the testing with sums a finance team can open in Excel.
4. **Brand.** Ink #0B0D12, Bone #F2EEE6, Ember #FF4F1F (the only accent). Fraunces for display at opsz 144, Geist for text, Geist Mono for figures and labels. The mark is an ink or bone ring (the supplier's quote) over an ember disc (the should-cost). The sliver where the disc escapes the ring is the gap Procis finds.
5. **World.** Low-key macro still life: the raw materials of packaging (PET preforms, aluminium coil, kraft board, film) under one hard raking key light on near-black. Preamble below.
6. **What it must not look like.** PackQuant (kraft brown with signal green), and none of the earlier FINGERPRINTS rows (the registry is empty at build time).
7. **Proof available.** Only the owner's figures: Packfora impact figures 2026, the Unilever call quote, the ICRA freight line and the board. Nothing else is invented. The claim-check table is labelled Illustrative, as the copy says.
8. **Constraints.** No em dashes, no scroll cues, no section counters, no text in images, image spend under $3.

## Journey (what they learn)

Hero (the promise) → the moment (letters arriving, nobody can tell true from padded) → the claim check (one letter taken apart) → index watch (the same discipline on the way down) → **your number** (they put in their own spend and see what the gap is worth) → the agents and the guarantees → partnership (fair to the supplier) → **the six weeks** (exactly what they get, week by week, for a fixed fee) → **before you forward this** (the answers their CISO, CFO, IT, suppliers and team will need) → why us (the proof) → the ask.

## Selling itself (no presenter in the room)

The owner's standing instruction is that the page "needs to sell itself". A sceptical CPO reads it alone and then forwards it. So the page does the work a salesperson would do, in this order:

1. **It proves the method on one example.** The claim check takes one letter apart in front of them.
2. **It lets them own the number.** The calculator (4b) uses their spend and their recovery rate. Its output is their figure, not our claim, and it is labelled as arithmetic, not a promise.
3. **It removes the unknowns about the engagement.** The six weeks (6b) state the scope, what they hand over, what comes back each week, and the fee terms.
4. **It answers the objections before the forward.** The five rows in 6c are open, with nothing to click, so the reader can quote them to the people who will ask.
5. **It shows the proof, then asks once.** The Packfora figures and the board come next, then one button.

## Feeling curve

```
1  Recognition   their own materials, lit like evidence, and a promise that sounds like an audit: "Every price, proven."
2  Unease        the stack of sealed letters; resin, energy, palm, freight, each "is up" landing like a knock at the door
3  Clarity       (the peak) one letter's +8.8% taken apart driver by driver, verdicts stamping, the mark snapping into the logo at +4.5%
4  Vindication   the rockets-and-feathers chart draws itself and the ember gap is money they can take back
4b Ownership     their own spend on a slider; the ring grows with it, the disc swells with the value, and at full recovery it locks into the logo
5  Control       six agents slide past, and the last item is three plain promises about who stays in charge
6  Fairness      the page turns to daylight bone; two copies of the same breakdown slide into alignment
6b Clarity       still in daylight, the six weeks light up one by one and end on the fee terms
6c Readiness     back to ink; five plain answers they can forward without editing
7  Confidence    real figures count up once, with their source and the board under them
8  Resolve       everything stops: one category, one number, one button that leans toward the hand
```

Adjacent states differ throughout. Unease (2) is deliberately quiet, so that the peak has something to arrive from.

## The peak

**Act 3, the claim check.** It has the largest span (4.6 viewport heights), the only bespoke engine and the authored silence before it. The visitor would tell a friend: "a supplier's +8.8% letter got taken apart line by line, and when the last line was tested the logo snapped together at the real number."

**Authored silence.** The first 8% of act 3 is deliberately almost empty: only the bare ring of the instrument draws itself on ink. This is intended, not dead scroll.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where a supplier's +8.8% letter gets taken apart line by line and the logo snaps together at the verdict.

## Grammar decision: filmic one-shot

Everything stays in the ink family with colour drift between acts, and there are no hard cuts. The one exception is the turn to bone in act 6, which is driven by the same drift so it reads as daylight arriving rather than a new page. The other seven grammars lost for these reasons:

- **Chaptered editorial** bans media above the fold, and this hero is the photographic world.
- **Live surface** suits a tool demo with real UI, but the product is a method and a number, not a screen.
- **Continuous world** needs a real geography to fly through; packaging spend has none.
- **Typographic poster** bans the photography that makes the materials real.
- **Gallery** is for a range of products, and this page is an argument.
- **Split stage** needs every beat to be a binary; the agents and proof beats are not.
- **Rhythmic cutlist** has the wrong energy for a sceptical senior buyer who wants to check the sums.

## Signature move: the ring and the disc

The brand mark is a live instrument. The ring is what the supplier asked for and the disc is what the cost supports.

- It starts concentric: you cannot see the gap.
- In the claim check, each tested driver slides the disc out of the ring by the share of the gap it accounts for.
- When the last driver is tested, the instrument lands exactly on the logo's own geometry (disc centre offset 39.6 on a 61.5 ring), which is +8.8% asked against +4.5% cost-justified.
- The nav mark mirrors the instrument through the whole page: concentric at the top, separating during the claim check, and in lockup from then on.

The rows reuse the same grammar at small scale: an outline bar for the claim and an ember bar for what the index supports.

## Style preamble (verbatim on every image)

> Macro still-life product photography, 100mm macro lens on medium format. One hard raking key light from the left at a very low angle, no fill, deep falloff into true black. Colour grade: near-black ink with a faint cool blue cast in the shadows, warm bone-white highlights, one small ember-orange glint. Visible surface texture, fine film grain, a few dust specks, honest imperfection. Photographic realism. NOT 3D render, NOT CGI, NOT illustration, no digital glow, no plastic sheen. Absolutely no text, no labels, no logos, no letters, no numbers anywhere.

That is eleven acts, with no device family twice in a row and no scrub acts (there is no footage). The total grew from about 15 to about 20.5 viewport heights with the three selling beats. The peak's 4.6 is still the largest span, and the calculator is the second-strongest moment.

The nav bar turns bone with an ink wordmark while the page is in daylight (partnership and the six weeks), driven by the page's background, so it never sits as a grey band over bone.

## Reduced motion

The hero planes are still and the headline is present at load. The claim check is shown resolved: every verdict stamped, the instrument in lockup, the numbers final. The chart is drawn with the gap filled. The rail becomes a scrollable region (engine floor), with the agents heading as its first item. Counts show their final values. The calculator is live and updates at once, with no easing. The six weeks are shown complete, every node lit and the fee line present. The question rows are shown, with an opacity fade only. Opacity changes stay; position changes go.
